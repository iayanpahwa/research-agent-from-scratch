## Answer

**Who coined the term:**
Simon Willison independently popularized the term "prompt injection" in September 2022. The term was initially used on Twitter by @himbodhisattva in May 2022, but Willison is credited with popularizing it.

**How it differs from jailbreaking:**

- **Prompt Injection**: Exploits an AI model's inability to differentiate between system instructions and user inputs. The attack tricks the model by embedding malicious instructions within regular user input.

- **Jailbreaking**: Bypasses the AI model's safeguards and safety mechanisms directly to get it to perform prohibited tasks.

The key distinction is that prompt injection targets a fundamental design limitation (inability to distinguish instruction types), while jailbreaking targets the safety guardrails themselves. However, some prompt injection attacks can involve jailbreaking, so the two techniques overlap.
